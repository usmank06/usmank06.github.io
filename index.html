AI Chatbots vs. Human Interaction

2025 ACM @ UTA research · MavBuddy 20study participants

Do AI chatbots actually make websites easier to use? Our team of four built MavBuddy into MavGrades, UTA’s course-data site, to find out.

  • GPT-4.1 agent with tools: it checks grade history for classes and professors before answering
  • Prompt built from a review of 20+ academic papers, then refined through user trials
  • Compared chatbot and human help on usability, trust and satisfaction with 20 participants
  • Course info felt clearer and picking sections took less effort
  • Human oversight and domain-specific tuning still mattered most for trust
